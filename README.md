# JElib — Asset Browser for Houdini

Browse, preview and drop your material, model and HDRI libraries straight into Houdini — SOPs and Solaris, with Redshift, Octane and Karma materials built for you.

**Requires Houdini 20.5, 21 or 22** (Windows, macOS, Linux).

## Get JElib

- **Free** — 1,000-asset limit: [joakims.gumroad.com/l/xmdgmm](https://joakims.gumroad.com/l/xmdgmm)
- **Paid** — no limit, plus Relink Files and Collect Files: same page, choose the Paid version.

The Releases on this page carry the **Free** edition zip. The Paid edition is delivered through Gumroad only.

## Install

1. Unzip.
2. Run `Install_JElib_Windows.bat` (Windows) or `Install_JElib_macOS.command` (macOS), or `"<Houdini>/bin/hython" setup_jelib.py`.
3. Restart Houdini and open **JElib → Asset Browser**.

## Update

Inside Houdini: **JElib → Check for Updates…**. The Free edition downloads and installs the new version in place; the Paid edition opens your Gumroad library, then installs the downloaded zip. Re-running the installer from a new zip works too.
